1. About Vegapay LMS

Vegapay LMS is a cloud-native, API-first credit lifecycle and loan management platform that enables banks, NBFCs, and their fintech partners to launch and operate credit products, including credit cards, credit lines on UPI, and EMI and loan products. The platform manages the complete account lifecycle, covering customer onboarding and account creation, limit management, real-time transaction authorization, billing and statement generation, interest, fee, and tax computation, repayments, delinquency and collections, and general ledger accounting and regulatory reporting. Every program is driven by configuration, so each bank or partner can define its own product construct, billing framework, fees, and policies, and go live quickly within the bank's risk and compliance framework.

3.2 37-Day Inactivity Closure

If an account is not activated within 30 days of creation, the customer is contacted for consent to activate it. If no consent is received, the account is closed within the next 7 working days at no cost to the customer.
